# N71/S8000: SN2400 500 mA charging and Apple PCIe local register 0x210

Physical verification on the real iPhone 6s (Samsung A9 S8000),
17 November 2026. Linux RAM kernel remains unchanged; stock iOS
encrypted backup was verified before staging the PCIe probe.

## Battery: stop repeating expired 90 mA sessions

The battery gauge initially reported **45%, Discharging, -232 mA**:
the bounded SN2400 one-hour session had expired and restored its
original input limit to **90 mA**. The already-proven owner-only
300 mA guarded lease restored Charging. With LCD brightness 420,
12 samples averaged only **+39 mA** net battery charge.

The verified host USB descriptor advertises a **500 mA** bus budget.
The stock-independent owner-developed SN2400 module already
explicitly allows 500 mA input, with the same 2-second battery/
temperature/VBUS health checks and 30..3600 second automatic
rollback. This is an *input limit*, not the cell's charge-current
setpoint and not evidence of USB fast-charge negotiation.

A **single 30-second, supervised 500 mA trial** was performed:
14 consecutive positive battery-current samples averaged
**+264,571 µA** (range **+41,000 to +328,000 µA**). SN2400 measured
VBUS approximately **4.775–4.80 V** under load; battery temperature
was **35.5 °C**. The trial was returned to the 300 mA safe
profile afterward.

After verifying that response, an owner-supervised, bounded
**500 mA / 3600 s** lease was enabled and the controller confirmed
`APPLIED input_uA=500000`; it retains automatic VBUS and battery
health shutdown and expiry rollback. The battery then climbed from
46% to **49%**, with a later +192 mA net battery reading at
**normal LCD brightness 1023**. These are finite observations,
not a guarantee of sustained charging to 100%.

Private Dell-only diagnostic
`n71_charge_preflight_before_hw_20261117_PRIVATE.py --ensure`
is used before future experiments. It fails closed on an invalid
board, kernel-module checksum, absent USB, battery state or lease age
and requests a renewal only during operator-invoked hardware work
when at least 2400 of 3600 seconds have elapsed. It is **not** a
persistent background charger or an unattended infinite renewal.

Do not silently lift the USB 500 mA configuration budget, alter the
SN2400 charge-current/voltage registers, bypass the thermal/VBUS
watchdog, or claim a wall fast-charge negotiation has happened.

## Apple port startup: the original 0x210 handling is nontrivial

The original Apple ARM64 port **vslot1504** at
`AppleS800xPCIe +0x172c` calls its local read method
**vslot2000** with register offset `0x210`, conditionally skips
the next call if the result is zero, otherwise calls local write
**vslot2008** with **the exact same 32-bit value** and offset.
Constructed original S8000 vtables, source instructions and private
kernelcache all passed
`tools/n71_s8000_stock_port_0210_source_gate.py`.
This proves the software operation, **not** the electrical semantics
of the register or whether it has write-one-to-clear behavior.

The new real ARM64 kernel module
`experimental/port0/n71_s8000_port1_0210_ro.c` was built,
verified and run on the iPhone **once**, with a single read of the
already-active WLAN port's local register. It returned:

| Native WLAN PCIe port1 register | Physical value |
| --- | --- |
| Local `0x210` | **`0x0001fc0f`** |
| Local control `0x80` | `0x00000001` |
| Local link/status `0x88` | `0x00000005` |
| Shared root port1 ID5 | `0x00000001` |

The read-only module unloaded normally. Wi-Fi carrier remained
`1`, storage `pcie_link0` stayed OFF, no NAND block device appeared,
and the gauge remained net-positive (subsequent +179 mA reading).
**Do not replay Apple's same-value write to 0x210 under Linux** until
the local status/acknowledgment bits have been fully qualified.

## Remaining critical path

1. Reverse the actual **DART0 `Fact`** and **NVMMU0 `Fact`**
   callback implementation and its power/clock side effects.
2. Qualify stock S8000 port0 PHY startup, the `3` / `7` root
   control commands and local 0x210 status semantics.
3. Only then consider an owner-attended port0 bring-up with
   NAND PERST held low and proven DMA isolation.

No NAND write, storage reset release, or additional port0 power-on
was performed in this study. Private binaries, charge logs,
backup and device identifiers remain on the authorized Dell.
